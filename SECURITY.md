# Security policy

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in a Pray product or service, please tell us privately. Do not open a public issue.

Report it to: devops@pray.com

Please include what you found, where, and steps to reproduce it. Do not access data that is not yours, and do not disrupt our services while testing.

We will acknowledge your report and keep you informed. Thank you for helping keep our community safe.
